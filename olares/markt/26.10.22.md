# Settings only through the profile
v26.10.22: The settings left the navigation. The single gear at the bottom left looked louder than the work; settings now open only from the profile at the top right — My account for everyone, Organisation settings for owners and admins. The search in the top bar finds them too: type "password" or "properties". Set bookmarks are muted instead of gold; in the bar, where everything is bookmarked anyway, they only appear on hover.

## Deutsch
